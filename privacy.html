Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

No Coast ("the App") is operated by Seven Notes Private Limited ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what the App collects, why, and what you can do about it. No Coast is for adults 18 and over.

No Account Details

We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and you don't sign in. The first time you open the App, it creates a random identifier and a secret for your device. They are not linked to your name, your Apple ID, or any hardware identifier.

What We Store on Our Servers

No Coast is a weekly coach, so it needs to remember what you tell it. We store your goal, your conversations with the coach, the moves you set, what you report each day and each week, and the record built from them. This content is encrypted with a key unique to your account.

We also store your coaching schedule and settings (such as your check-in day, time, and time zone) and a small amount of technical data to keep the service running and prevent abuse.

Because the coach is open-ended, please don't share information you wouldn't want processed as described here, such as financial or health details.

How the AI Coach Uses Your Words

The coach is powered by a third-party AI provider. What you write to the coach, along with the earlier context it needs (your goal and past weeks), is sent to our AI provider to produce the coach's replies. We only use AI providers that do not use this data to train their models.

To help the coach remember earlier weeks, Cloudflare Workers AI converts your lines into numerical representations ("embeddings"). These are stored in Cloudflare Vectorize, labeled only with your random account identifier and coaching round.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App sends us a push token so the coach can remind you. Reminders are delivered through Expo's push notification service and may include the words of the move you set.

Helping Improve the Coach

Settings has a switch called "Help improve the coach." It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the founder may read your conversations with the coach to improve it. You can turn it off at any time.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier linked to your App Store purchase. We do not receive your name, email, payment method, or Apple ID.

Analytics

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage events, such as finishing onboarding, to understand how the App is used. These events do not include what you write to the coach.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising or marketing. We do not show ads. The services listed below process data only to run the App on our behalf.

Third-Party Services

AI provider: Generates the coach's replies. It may not use your data to train its models.

Cloudflare: Hosting, database, and embeddings. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

Expo: Push notification delivery. expo.dev/privacy

RevenueCat: Subscription status. revenuecat.com/privacy

PostHog: Anonymous analytics. posthog.com/privacy

Apple: Payments. apple.com/legal/privacy

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. You can delete it at any time in the App: Settings → Delete account. This deletes everything we hold about you right away, including the embeddings, and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

If you uninstall the App without deleting your account, your data stays on our servers. To have it deleted, email us at hello@sevennotesapps.com.

If you used an earlier version of No Coast, its onboarding data still expires as that version promised: the interview transcript after 7 days, and the goal summary and messages after 12 months. If you update, a copy of your goal summary may be kept, encrypted, with your new account so the coach can start from it; it is deleted when you delete your account.

Anonymous analytics data is kept for up to 12 months.

Age

No Coast is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the App, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the effective date.
